About Kitui Housing Dashboard

Our Mission

The Kitui Housing Dashboard is a comprehensive web application designed to visualize and provide information about affordable housing projects in Kitui County, Kenya. Our platform makes it easy for residents, potential homebuyers, county officials, and developers to explore housing opportunities across the county.

Kitui County Housing Context

Kitui County has been actively participating in Kenya's Affordable Housing Program, part of the Big 4 Agenda. The county government has partnered with various developers to deliver affordable housing units to residents. Projects range from small-scale developments to large estates with thousands of units.

Key initiatives include:

  • Boma Yangu Kwa Ngendu Estate in Kitui Town
  • Mwingi Kwa Ngendu Affordable Housing Project
  • Manyenyoni Affordable Housing Project
  • Civil Servants Housing Schemes

Inspiration from Boma Yangu

This dashboard is inspired by the Boma Yangu platform, which provides a national view of affordable housing projects across Kenya. Our focus is specifically on Kitui County, providing detailed, localized information that helps users make informed decisions about housing in the region.

Features

  • 🗺️ Interactive Map: Explore projects on an interactive map powered by OpenStreetMap
  • 🔍 Smart Search: Find projects quickly with autocomplete search functionality
  • 📊 Statistics: View aggregated data and visualizations of housing projects
  • 📝 Detailed Information: Access comprehensive project details including status, units, pricing, and amenities
  • ⭐ Favorites: Save your preferred projects for quick access
  • 📱 Responsive Design: Access the dashboard on any device

Technology

Built with modern web technologies:

  • Backend: Flask (Python)
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
  • Maps: Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap
  • UI Framework: Bootstrap 5
  • Data: JSON-based storage (easily migratable to database)
